ACE-Step (vendored runtime)
===========================

This directory contains a vendored copy of ACE-Step v1.5 used for local
text-to-music generation.

Upstream project: https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step
License: Apache License 2.0

The model weights (checkpoints/) are NOT part of this plugin package; they are
downloaded or copied separately into the compute root at runtime.
